This is where it all starts. We'll spend the afternoon making Fish Amok together — Cambodia's most beloved dish. No experience needed, just bring your hands and your appetite.

We'll cover:
- How to make kroeung paste from scratch
- How to fold banana leaf cups
- The steaming technique that makes amok so special

All ingredients and equipment will be provided. Spots are limited to 12 so everyone gets hands-on time.
