ingredients:
  meats:
    - 500g pork shoulder or loin, thinly sliced
  produce:
    - Pickled daikon and carrot, for serving
    - Cucumber slices, for serving
  pantry:
    - 3 cloves garlic, minced
    - 200ml coconut milk
    - 1 tbsp fish sauce
    - 1 tsp sugar
    - Pinch of white pepper
    - 2 cups broken jasmine rice (or regular jasmine rice), cooked
equipment:
  - Charcoal grill or grill pan
  - Mixing bowl
  - Rice cooker or pot
steps:
  - Combine garlic, coconut milk, fish sauce, sugar, and pepper in a bowl to make the marinade.
  - Add pork slices and marinate for at least 30 minutes (overnight in the fridge is best).
  - Grill pork over high heat for 2–3 minutes per side until caramelized and cooked through. A charcoal grill gives the best flavor.
  - Serve over a bowl of warm broken rice with pickled vegetables and cucumber on the side.
---

Bai sach chrouk is sold from carts across Phnom Penh starting before sunrise. The coconut milk in the marinade gives the pork a slightly sweet, caramelized crust when it hits the grill. It is simple food — just a few ingredients — but it is deeply satisfying.

Broken rice is traditional but regular jasmine rice works perfectly too. The pickled vegetables are essential — they cut through the richness of the pork.
